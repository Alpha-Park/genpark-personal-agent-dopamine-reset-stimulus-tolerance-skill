# Personal Agent Dopamine Reset Stimulus Tolerance Skill

Designed for **OpenClaw**, **Hermes**, and autonomous personal AI agents to protect human agency, quantify modern supernormal stimulus overload, model striatal dopamine receptor down-regulation, and guide structured neurochemical resets.

## Agent In-Context Capabilities

- **Supernormal Stimulus Quantification**: Calculates weighted impact of short-form algorithmic video feeds, infinite scrolling, processed foods, and high-frequency stimulants.
- **Effort Discounting Modeling**: Computes the exact multiplier by which low-stimulation deep work feels disproportionately difficult.
- **Phased Reset Architecture**: Generates actionable, low-friction sensory blackout protocols with clear withdrawal trajectories.

## Tools Summary

1. `calculate_hedonic_tolerance`: Computes cumulative stimulus units, D2 receptor availability, and effort friction.
2. `synthesize_dopamine_reset_protocol`: Prescribes a day-by-day stimulus elimination and restorative activity plan.
